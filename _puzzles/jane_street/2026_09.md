My experience with this puzzle was very similar to my experience with the June 2026 puzzle; both were of a similar nature — figure out what to do with some cryptic info — which also happens to be the kind of puzzle that Fred Vu and I like to collaborate on. Much like in June, I was occupied with a career event, so most of the heavy lifting was done by Fred and I only managed to contribute a couple of minor pieces of information. This was a pretty fun one and I enjoyed finding the solution with Fred as always, but it was also another relatively easy solve that left us wishing for a little more.

**Difficulty:** 4/10

## The approach:

The meaning of the very first title on the back of the album cover was clear to us almost immediately: as soon as we read *Buddy Holly*, Fred and I both thought of the band Weezer, who have a well-known song by that name; that this was a homophone with "wheezer", which could describe someone who'd just run a 5k, was hard to miss. The fact that we had to add a "h" to Weezer, together with the title of the puzzle and the three stickers on the bottom of the album sleeve all clearly having an unexpected letter inserted somewhere, was enough for us to form a hypothesis about what we had to do with each clue.

Our hunch was confirmed by a couple other relatively easy examples that stood out to us; for example "Hurt (In a Fender Bender)" references *Hurt* by Johnny Cash, adding an "r" gives "Johnny Crash", and crash is another term for a fender bender. So, we concluded that each clue was a modified song title (each being a single from some album), and that a letter could be added somewhere in the name of each song's artist to form something pertaining to whatever had been added to the song's title to modify it.

We set about solving as many clues as possible, each time keeping track of the letter which had to be added. We didn't solve all of the clues, but we solved enough for it to become clear that the message being spelled out was "Help! (Our drummer is out sick)". This clearly matched the form of all other clues in the puzzle so far, and it was obvious that this referenced the song "Help!" by the Beatles. According to the pattern, we had to add one letter to "Beatles" to form something related to "our drummer is out sick", and the obvious answer was **"Beatless"**, which was the solution to the puzzle.

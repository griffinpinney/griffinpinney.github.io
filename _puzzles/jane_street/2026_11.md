I don't even know what to say anymore. This was a brilliantly constructed puzzle, almost on par *Timely Journey* from January 2026 (probably my favourite ever Jane Street puzzle). In a pre-AI world, this month's puzzle would've earned difficulty and enjoyability ratings of at least 9/10 from me, and at first glance, it looked like this was going to be the case. Fred Vu and I exchanged excited messages, looking forward to what appeared to be the most challenging and rewarding puzzle in a while, but before we knew it, the leaderboard was populated with more than 10 names, each of whom had clearly one-shotted the puzzle with AI... Even a year ago, this puzzle probably would've lasted the entire month without being solved by more than 100 teams, but this time, the puzzle had almost as many solvers by the end of its release day. I know there's nothing that forces Fred and I to follow suit, but we used to genuinely love the thrill of competing for top positions on the leaderboard, and it's sad that this competition has now been more or less ruined. I praise whoever it was at Jane Street who created this excellent puzzle; my poor ratings below simply reflect the sad state of affairs that have made competing for top positions on the leaderboard neither difficult nor enjoyable.
